What's Ceol?

This site allows you to track a list of traditional Irish tunes you know and play.

Its main function is to be your personal tune list. You can add tunes you want to learn, or already know, and sync your Tunebook from TheSession.org. You can then sort them so you're learning the right tunes first: the tunes people really play in your sessions.

The site also tracks basic info about sessions, like the address and the recurrence, i.e. how often it happens (like "Every Thursday"). Sessions can be linked to a session record on TheSession.org, and we can import the details from there.

Each time you hold a session, you can log the tunes you played on this site. Logs have a date, a list of tunes (grouped into sets), and a little other info.

Logging happens on the live logger, a screen built for use during the session itself: type a few letters of a tune name and it matches instantly, several people can log together and see each other's changes in real time, and it keeps working even when the pub Wi-Fi doesn't. See The Live Logger for a full tour.

You can think of this site as a sort of companion to TheSession.org. Info about tunes and sessions all over the world is there. This site adds the social component of tracking what tunes are really played where.

If you'd like to add your session to our list, feel free!

Key Features

  • Personal Tune List - Track tunes you know, are learning, or want to learn
  • Live Session Logging - Record what tunes are played, in real time, together with the other players
  • Tune Popularity - See which tunes are most commonly played at sessions
  • Search by Notes - Anywhere you can search for a tune by name, you can also type a few bars in ABC (e.g. "GED BED") to find it by its notation — for when you have the tune but not the name
  • TheSession.org Integration - Import tunes from your tunebook and link to tune details
  • Mobile-Friendly - Use it at the session on your phone

On Your Phone

On an iPhone, Ceol is an app: search for Ceol on the App Store. There's no Android app, but the website installs onto an Android phone's home screen and behaves like one: it opens full-screen with its own icon, keeps you signed in, and works offline the same way. To install it:

  1. Open ceol.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app), then Install.

Chrome sometimes offers an Install bar at the bottom of the page when you first visit; tapping that does the same thing. In Samsung Internet it's under the menu, at Add page to → Home screen. If you added Ceol from ceol.io before the move, remove that icon and add it again from ceol.app.

On an iPhone without the app, Safari can do the same: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Places

Every session's address starts with its town, like ceol.app/sessions/austin/mueller. The town's own address, ceol.app/sessions/austin, lists every session there; a city's page also lists the towns around it. Tap a session's town in the sessions list to get there. Site admins look after the list of places, so if a town is spelled wrong or belongs inside a nearby city, ask one.

Festivals

A festival has one address, like ceol.app/sessions/oflahertys. In the week before a festival and the month after it, that address takes you to this year's festival. The rest of the time it shows a list of every year, so you can pick one. On a year's page you can switch to another year from the menu beside its name.

Each year is its own session, with its own venue, dates, schedule and logs. A festival's admins start a new year by copying an old one: it brings the venue, timezone, settings and admins, but not the schedule or the logs.

Without an Account

You can look around Ceol without signing in, on the website and in the iPhone app. You can browse sessions, their tunes and their logs, follow a night's log while it's being written, look up any tune and its notation, and use Listen to find out what tune is playing. You need an account to keep your own list of tunes, join a session, or log or change anything. Wherever one is needed, Ceol asks you to sign in, and afterwards brings you back to where you were. Creating an account is free: enter your email address and we'll send you a link.

Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from Me → Delete Account. That removes your login, your tune list and instruments, and your contact details straight away. Your name stays on the sessions you were part of, the way it would for anyone a session admin adds, and the tunes logged at those sessions stay in their logs.
