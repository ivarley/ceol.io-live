What's Ceol?

Ceol is a companion to TheSession.org for people who play Irish traditional music. TheSession.org has the tunes. Ceol records the tunes you know, the sessions you go to, and the tunes that were played at each session.

Your personal tune list is the main feature. You can add the tunes you know and the tunes you want to learn. You can also copy your tunebook from TheSession.org. Ceol shows you which tunes are played most at your sessions, so you can learn those first.

Each session has a page. The page shows the venue, the schedule, the tunes played there, and a log for each night. A log lists the tunes that were played, in sets, and the people who were there. You write the log with the live logger during the session. Several people can write the same log at the same time.

You can add your session to Ceol. If you are new to sessions, read What's a Session? first.

Key Features

  • My Tune List - Record the tunes you know, the tunes you are learning, and the tunes you want to learn, for each instrument you play
  • Sessions - How to find a session, join it, or add your own. Each session has Tunes, Logs, The Live Logger and People
  • Places - Each session belongs to a town. A town's page lists the sessions in that town
  • Festivals - A festival has one address and a session for each year
  • Listen - The iPhone app can tell you the name of the tune that is playing. It can also log a night for you
  • Searching by Notes - Type a few notes to find a tune when you do not know its name
  • TheSession.org - What Ceol takes from TheSession.org: tunes, notation, settings, popularity, your tunebook, and session listings

If something is wrong or broken, see Contact & Support. The Release Notes list recent changes.
