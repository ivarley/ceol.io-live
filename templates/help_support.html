Contact & Support

For anything about Ceol, the website or the iPhone app, email support@ceol.app. A person reads every message, usually within a couple of days.

Reporting a page

If you see something offensive, wrong or spam, or something that's broken, use Report:

  • In the app, tap ··· at the top right of any screen, then Report.
  • On the website, open the menu at the top right, then Report. On a phone, it's on Me.

Reports go straight to support. Content that breaks the rules below is removed, and anyone who keeps posting it loses their account.

The rules

Ceol is for the music. Anything you add (session names and descriptions, notes, names, logs) must not be:

  • abusive, hateful, threatening or sexual;
  • about a private person without their agreement, such as their phone number or address;
  • spam, advertising, or made up.

Your account and your data

  • Change your details on Me.
  • Delete your account from Me → Delete Account.
  • What Ceol keeps, and why, is in the Privacy Policy.
